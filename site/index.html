SOTA Indicator Methodology

SOTA Indicator Methodology & Scoring Weights

1. Risk-Adjusted Momentum (35%)

Combines 12-1M and 6-1M Momentum (Jegadeesh & Titman factor excluding the most recent month to avoid short-term mean reversion) with 1Y Sortino Ratio to reward upside convexity over downside volatility.

2. Trend Quality & Relative Strength (25%)

Uses Frog-in-the-Pan (FIP) smoothness (Da, Gurun & Warachka), 52W High Proximity (George & Hwang), cross-sectional RS Rating (1–99), and Stage 2 MA Regime (Price > SMA50 > SMA200).

3. Institutional Flow & Alpha (20%)

Tracks 60D Up/Down Volume Ratio (> 1.15 signals accumulation), 20D/90D Relative Volume, and 6M Alpha vs SPY.

4. Medium-Term Fundamentals (20%)

Balances momentum with operating backing: Revenue Growth YoY, Earnings Growth YoY, Return on Equity (ROE), and PEG Ratio discipline.
